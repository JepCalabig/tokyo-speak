Particle Nuances

Particles are tiny cameras. They decide what the sentence is looking at, and how. Below, each particle gets the same treatment: what it points at, what kind of information it marks, one image, a default use, a sharper use, and the mix-up people actually make.


wa vs ga

wa

Points at: the thing already on the table.
Marks: old information, or contrast.
Image: a card already laid down. "As for this..."

Default
watashi wa gakusei desu.
As for me, I'm a student.

Sharper use: contrast
koohii wa nomimasu. ocha wa nomimasen.
Coffee I drink. Tea I don't.

Often confused with: ga
Job: topic / contrast
Feel: known, soft, or "this one vs that one"
Mix-up: treating every subject as wa


ga

Points at: the doer, the thing that exists, or the item you are identifying.
Marks: new information, identification, sometimes an exclusive pick.
Image: a flashlight. It points at one thing and says look at this.

Default
neko ga imasu.
There is a cat.

Sharper use: identification
dare ga ikimasu ka.
Who is going?

tanaka-san ga ikimasu.
Tanaka is the one going.

The train set
densha ga kimashita.
A train has arrived. / Here comes a train.

kono densha ga ueno ni ikimasu ka.
Is this the train that goes to Ueno?
You are picking this train out of several.

kono densha wa ueno ni ikimasu ka.
Does this train go to Ueno?
The train is already the topic. Default platform question.

Often confused with: wa
Job: doer / new info / "this one"
Feel: spotlight, announcement, selection
Mix-up: using ga just because something is the grammatical subject


o

Points at: the thing the verb acts on, or the space you move across.
Marks: a relationship between action and target.
Image: the tip of an arrow. What the verb touches.

Default
hon o yomimasu.
I read a book.

Sharper use: path / space
hashi o watarimasu.
I cross the bridge.

sora o tobu.
fly through the sky

Often confused with: ga after wanting / seeming / potential
mizu ga hoshii.
I want water.

eiga ga mitai.
I want to see a movie.

Job: object / path
Feel: "do this to that"
Mix-up: mapping every English object onto o


ni

Points at: landing spot, time point, receiver, place of existence, purpose.
Marks: a relationship: to where, at when, to whom, in order to.
Image: a pin. You arrive there, exist there, or hand something there.

Default
tookyoo ni ikimasu.
I go to Tokyo.

sanji ni aimasu.
I'll meet at 3.

tomodachi ni agemashita.
I gave it to a friend.

eki ni imasu.
I'm at the station.

tabe ni ikimasu.
I go in order to eat.

Sharper use: agent in passive
sensei ni shikararemashita.
I got scolded by the teacher.

Often confused with: e and de
tookyoo e ikimasu.
I head toward Tokyo.

Job: destination / time / receiver / existence / purpose
Feel: landing point
Mix-up: "place = ni" for every location


de

Points at: the stage of the action, the tool, the material, the cause, the scope.
Marks: a relationship: where it is done, what it is done with.
Image: a stage and a prop. The action happens here, using this.

Default
kissaten de benkyou shimasu.
I study at a cafe.

basu de ikimasu.
I go by bus.

ki de tsukurimasu.
I make it out of wood.

Sharper use: cause
kaze de gakkou o yasumimashita.
I missed school because of a cold.

Often confused with: ni
eki ni imasu.
I am at the station.

eki de aimasu.
We meet at the station.

Job: place of action / means / material / cause
Feel: the scene of doing
Mix-up: using ni for any "at"


to

Points at: a partner in a list, a companion, quoted speech or thought, a natural condition.
Marks: a relationship: with, and, that.
Image: a tie. It binds A to B.

Default
pan to gyuunyuu o kaimashita.
I bought bread and milk.

tomodachi to hanashimasu.
I talk with a friend.

iku to iimashita.
He said he would go.

muzukashii to omoimasu.
I think it's difficult.

Sharper use: when X happens, Y naturally follows
haru ni naru to sakura ga sakimasu.
When it becomes spring, the cherry blossoms bloom.

Often confused with: ya and ni
ringo to banana
apples and bananas

ringo ya banana
apples, bananas, and so on

tomodachi to iku.
go together with a friend

tomodachi ni au.
meet a friend

Job: and / with / quote / natural if
Feel: pairing
Mix-up: translating every "with" as to


mo

Points at: an extra item added to a set already in mind.
Marks: addition, sometimes "even."
Image: one more thing dropped into the basket. "This too."

Default
watashi mo ikimasu.
I will go too.

pan mo kaimashita.
I bought bread too.

Sharper use
ocha demo nomimashou.
Let's drink tea or something.

Often confused with: stacking it on top of wa / ga / o
Job: also / even
Feel: plus
Mix-up: forgetting that mo takes the seat of wa, ga, or o instead of sitting beside them


kara / made

Points at: starting point and ending point. After a clause, kara points at a reason.
Marks: a relationship: from-to, or because.
Image: a line segment. Start here, stop there.

Default
ku-ji kara go-ji made hatarakimasu.
I work from 9 to 5.

Sharper use: because
tsukareta kara nemasu.
I'll sleep because I'm tired.

Often confused with: node, which feels softer and more explanatory
Job: from / until / because
Feel: range, or "so..."
Mix-up: treating noun-kara and clause-kara as the same mental move


no

Points at: the link between nouns, or a clause turned into a noun-like chunk.
Marks: a relationship of belonging or kind; sometimes an explanation.
Image: glue. It sticks nouns together.

Default
watashi no hon
my book

nihongo no sensei
a Japanese teacher / a teacher of Japanese

taberu no ga suki desu.
I like eating.

Sharper use: soft explanation
isogashii no.
(It's that) I'm busy.

tomodachi no imouto no keitai
my friend's sister's phone

Often confused with: forcing every English of / 's into no
Job: link / nominalize / explain
Feel: one bundled unit
Mix-up: overusing no where Japanese would just compound or drop it


Extra distinctions

Potential verbs often take ga on the thing you can do.
kanji ga yomemasu.
I can read kanji.

Companion vs target
tomodachi to iku.
go with a friend

tomodachi ni au.
meet a friend

Particle + wa makes contrast.
tookyoo ni wa ikimasu. oosaka ni wa ikimasen.
To Tokyo I will go. To Osaka I won't.

Clause-final ga can mean "but."
ame ga futta ga, dekaketa.
It rained, but I went out.


Decision guide

Doer, existence, new info, which one? -> ga
Already the topic, or contrast? -> wa
Thing acted on, or space crossed? -> o
Arrive, time, receiver, exist, purpose? -> ni
Place of doing, tool, material, cause? -> de
Direction toward? -> e
And / with / quote? -> to
Also / even? -> mo
From / because? -> kara
Up to? -> made
Glue nouns or turn a phrase into a thing? -> no
